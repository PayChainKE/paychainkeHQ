Quick Answers

FAQ
How do I withdraw funds? expand_more
Navigate to your balance card, tap 'Withdraw', and select your linked bank or mobile money account. Transfers are processed instantly.
Is my money safe? expand_more
Yes. PayChain utilizes bank-grade 256-bit encryption and is fully licensed by the Central Bank. Your funds are held in secure escrow accounts.
How to increase my Trust Score? expand_more
Consistent transaction volume, timely repayments of advances, and positive customer feedback are the fastest ways to improve your score.
What are the transaction fees? expand_more
We offer a flat rate of 1.5% for all inward payments. Outward transfers to mobile money start from KES 25 depending on the amount.
Can I use PayChain offline? expand_more
You can receive payments via your merchant USSD code even without an internet connection. App features require data access.
How do I link a second store? expand_more
Go to Profile > Manage Business > Add New Branch. You can toggle between different business entities seamlessly within the same app.
