01problem
The problem

Kenyan merchants run their business on five disconnected tools.

  • Collections happen on M-Pesa. Payroll happens through a paybill and a spreadsheet, or cash in an envelope. Invoices are a Word template with no tax compliance behind them. Working capital means a chama, a shark loan, or nothing at all.
  • KRA now requires VAT-registered businesses to issue invoices through eTIMS — a real compliance obligation most merchants have no practical way to meet, and one most fintechs don't touch at all.
  • None of these tools verify who's actually on the other end. A fraud ring can register five merchant accounts on five stolen IDs, and nothing catches it until the money's already moved.

The result: a merchant's money, tax compliance and payroll all depend on tools that were never built to talk to each other — or to defend the business that depends on them.

02solution
The solution

One merchant account. Every money movement. Compliance built in, not bolted on.

  • PayChain is the merchant operating account for Kenyan SMEs: collect via M-Pesa and bank, pay staff and suppliers in bulk, invoice customers with real KRA eTIMS compliance, draw working capital, and hold a digital wallet — one dashboard, one mobile app, one ledger.
  • Most fintechs bolt on fraud controls after their first incident. PayChain shipped with them: velocity-limited payouts, new-device login alerts, and cross-account identity-document matching are live in production today, not a roadmap item.
  • Onboarding matches how Kenyan business actually works — a Sole Proprietorship and a PLC are asked for exactly the paperwork the law requires for that structure, not one generic upload box for everyone.
03product
Product

A merchant's entire money workflow, in one account.

  1. 01
    CollectM-Pesa STK Push, Till/QR, and direct bank rails via NCBA Open Banking — money lands in one balance, not five wallets.
  2. 02
    PayBulk Pay runs payroll and supplier payouts in a single batch, over M-Pesa B2C or bank transfer, reconciled automatically.
  3. 03
    InvoiceCustomer invoicing wired directly into KRA's eTIMS, so every invoice a merchant sends is tax-compliant the moment it's issued.
  4. 04
    GrowA Cash Advance product underwritten on a merchant's own transaction history, plus an opt-in digital wallet for merchants holding value in stablecoin.

Delivered as a web dashboard, a native mobile app, and an internal admin console for compliance and support — three products, one ledger underneath.

04moat
Why we win

We treat compliance and fraud prevention as the product, not a checkbox.

  • Business-type-aware KYB. A sole trader, a SACCO, and a PLC are legally different animals — each is asked for exactly the documents Kenyan law requires for that structure (CR12, business permit, National ID, KRA PIN certificate).
  • Automatic fraud screening on every account. Outbound transfers auto-lock for 15 minutes on suspicious velocity and alert our team; logins from an unrecognized device trigger an instant alert; identity documents are hashed and cross-checked against every other merchant on the platform, so identity reuse is caught at signup — before the money moves, not after.
  • This is infrastructure a competitor has to spend a year rebuilding after their first fraud incident. We built it before we needed to.

06model
Business model

We make money exactly when our merchants do.

  • Take-rate on payment volume — a per-transaction tariff on every collection and payout moving through a merchant's account.
  • FX spread — on wallet conversions between KES and stablecoin balance, for merchants who opt into the digital wallet.
  • Cash Advance fees — a fee on working capital drawn against a merchant's own verified transaction history.
  • [Optional: a subscription tier for advanced invoicing/compliance features, once usage data supports it]

No per-seat SaaS fee. Pricing scales with a merchant's own volume — free to try, and it grows only as their business does.
